
# MY PROFILE 
My name is **Sourav Kumar Sharma**, and I am currently a third-year student of the integrated B.Sc.–B.Ed. programme at the Indian Institute of Technology Ropar, belonging to the 2028 batch.

Coming from a lower-middle-class family, I have learned the importance of hard work, perseverance, self-discipline, and making the best use of available opportunities. My academic journey has been shaped by these values, reflected in my achievements of securing District Topper status in Class 10 and State Rank 9 in the Bihar Board Class 12 examinations.

As a student of science and teacher education, I am deeply interested in physics, innovative teaching methodologies, and educational technology. I aspire to make science learning more engaging, accessible, and meaningful for students. My academic experiences have encouraged me to explore student-centred teaching, conceptual learning, and the effective integration of technology into education.

Beyond academics, I am an active member of the official IIT Ropar Volleyball Team, where I continue to develop teamwork, leadership, discipline, and resilience. I am also interested in entrepreneurship and aspire to contribute to the education sector by developing innovative solutions that improve access to quality learning, particularly for students from small towns and financially constrained backgrounds.

My long-term vision is to combine science, education, and technology to create meaningful educational opportunities and contribute to transforming the learning experiences of students.
